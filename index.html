As Shot

A manual camera app for iPhone, and a "Share Clean Copy" action for the share sheet that removes metadata from photos before you send them. As Shot is free; As Shot Pro is a one-time in-app purchase.

Privacy policy

As Shot collects no data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no servers. Nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

The app asks for three permissions, each when first needed:

  • Camera, to take photos. The microphone is never used.
  • Add to Photos, to save your photos and RAW files. The app cannot read your existing library.
  • Location while using the app, to record where each photo was taken in the photo itself, as the built-in Camera app does. It is used only while the camera is open and stops in the background. It is on by default, and you can turn it off in As Shot's settings; the app then never asks for location. The location is stored only inside your own photos.

Settings are stored on your device only. So you can look at the photo you just took, the app keeps a copy of your last two photos in its own storage on your phone; they are deleted the next time the app starts.

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. When you buy or restore As Shot Pro, or start the free trial, the App Store processes it under Apple's privacy policy, and the app only checks with the App Store what you own. The developer never receives your payment details or your Apple Account.

Share Clean Copy sees only the photos you share with it. The cleaned copies are made on your device, in a temporary folder that is deleted when you finish. If a photo has a location and you tap "Show on map", the map is drawn by Apple Maps, which sends that location to Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Until you tap, no map is loaded and nothing is sent.

No part of the app or the extension connects to the internet apart from that optional map and the App Store for purchases.

Support

Questions, problems and suggestions: open an issue on GitHub (a free GitHub account is needed). Issues are public, so please don't attach photos that show where they were taken: share a clean copy, or describe the problem instead.
